Serve Our Wider Community

Unitarian Universalism is a tradition rooted in the practice of "transforming ourselves, our communities, and our world." In this spirit, members and friends of Emerson Chapel work for social justice in various capacities.

WE CAN

West County Community Action Network grew from the involvement of Emerson Chapel members in local Black Lives Matter and racial justice efforts. It is now an independent coalition of people who live and work in West County, St. Louis and who seek to promote justice for all. Learn more on the WE CAN Facebook page.

Pride

Emerson Chapel participates in St. Louis and St. Charles pride activities annually during June. Along with the other St. Louis area UU churches, we march in the STL Pridefest parade and we host a booth at Pride St. Charles in Frontier Park.

Serve Emerson Chapel

Unitarian Universalism is a tradition rooted in the practice of "transforming ourselves, our community, and our world." In this spirit, we have opportunities to transform ourselves through service to congregational life at Emerson Chapel.

Teaching Religious Education

Emerson Chapel has a strong tradition of creating meaningful opportunities for all age groups to grow spiritually through participation in religious education class and events. These religious education classes and events, however, rely largely on volunteers, who might serve on the Children's Religious Education team, the Adult Religious Education team, or simply help teach or prepare for individual classes.

Worship Team

As a lay-led congregation, Emerson Chapel relies on our members to organize and lead regular Sunday services. There are many roles you can play to help with worship, from acting as liturgist or worship leader, to assisting with greeting visitors or hospitality after the service.

Interested in learning more about these or other opportunities to serve Emerson Chapel? Please contact us by email (office@emersonuuchapel.org) or by phone at (636)-757-3633. We’ll connect you and the appropriate volunteer organizer, who will be happy to assist you. 

Womyn’s Web

For all women of the chapel, Womyn’s Web is a social group that provides a wonderful opportunity to get to know one another in a welcoming social setting, while touching on a wide variety of topics. We open with drinks and snacks brought by the attendees, then settle in for a discussion led by one of the group. Facilitation is on a volunteer basis. All are welcome to lead, participate or just come and be together.

Circle Suppers

Circle Suppers are a great way to socialize and meet new members and friends outside of church. Circle suppers are informal gatherings that bring people together to share good conversation and a meal.

Typically, a circle supper is a potluck-based dinner held in the host’s home where the host(s) provides the main dish and the guests volunteer to bring the appetizer, salad, dessert, side dishes, beverages, etc. However, the supper can be a potluck with a more open format, it can be at a restaurant if the host doesn’t have the space to host a dinner at their home, or it can even be at the church. Circle suppers can be adult-only or for families.

Meeting Time & Location

Emerson members often host circle suppers monthly but the time, location, and format depends on who is willing to host.
