Emerson Unitarian Universalist Chapel (“Emerson Chapel,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information our website, emersonuuchapel.org, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Contact and newcomer forms. When you fill out a form on our site, we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, and your message. These submissions are sent by email to our church office.
  • Pledge form. Our pledge form is hosted by Google Forms. Information you enter there is collected by Google on our behalf and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Member accounts. If you register for a member account, we store the details you provide, such as your name, username, and email address, so you can sign in to member-only pages and resources.
  • Newsletter. If you sign up for our newsletter, we use your email address to send you church news and announcements. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How we use your information

We use the information you share with us to respond to your questions, welcome newcomers, keep members informed, manage pledges and donations, and run the congregation’s ministries. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Donations

Online donations are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your full card or bank details. PayPal’s handling of your information is described in the PayPal Privacy Statement.

Cookies

Our site uses a small number of cookies that are needed for it to work, for example to keep you signed in to a member account. If you log in, WordPress sets cookies that last for two days, or two weeks if you choose “Remember Me.” Logging out removes them. We do not use advertising cookies.

Third-party services and embedded content

Some pages include content or links from other services, such as YouTube videos, Zoom for online services, PayPal for donations, and Google Forms. These services may collect information about you and set their own cookies when you interact with them, according to their own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. Registration for children’s religious education is handled by parents or guardians.

Keeping your information safe

Access to personal information is limited to church staff and volunteers who need it for their roles. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no website can guarantee complete security.

Your choices

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to remove you from our mailing lists. To make a request, contact the church office using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
