Emerson Chapel uses a few cookies, and one small note in your browser, so this website can work. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell information about your visit.

Cookies that keep the site working

When you sign in, WordPress stores cookies so you stay logged in. They last about two days, or about two weeks if you tick Remember Me. Logging out removes them. A short-lived test cookie checks that your browser accepts cookies at all. Member pages need these cookies. The site cannot keep you signed in without them.

The newsletter invitation

The homepage may show a pop-up asking you to join This Week at Emerson. If you close it or sign up, the browser saves a small note in local storage, which works like a cookie. The note is only “dismissed” or “subscribed,” plus the date. It has no name or email, and it is never sent to us. A closed invitation stays hidden for 30 days, then it can show again. After you subscribe, it stays hidden until you use Clear the newsletter reminder below, or clear this site’s data in your browser.

Other services

Some pages include YouTube, Zoom, PayPal, or Google Forms. Those services may set their own cookies when you use them. Their policies are linked from our Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookies, member pages will not be able to keep you logged in.

Manage your preferences

These buttons only change notes stored in this browser. They do not change your member account.
