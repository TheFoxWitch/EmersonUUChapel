Emerson Unitarian Universalist Chapel (“Emerson Chapel,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information our website, emersonuuchapel.org, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Contact and newcomer forms. When you fill out a form on our site (for example the Newcomer Information form), we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, and your message. These submissions are sent by email to our church office.
  • Commitment and pledge forms. Our current commitment form on the homepage is hosted by Microsoft Forms. Information you enter there is collected by Microsoft on our behalf and is subject to the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Some older pages may still link to a Google Form; those submissions are collected by Google on our behalf and are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • On-site forms. Forms that open in a dialog on our site (for example the Emerson Community Survey and the PayPal fees helper) send your answers by email to the church office. The form tool on our website may also keep a copy of those submissions in our website database so the office can review them. We keep those copies while they are useful for ministry and delete them when they are no longer needed, and we aim not to keep them longer than 24 months.
  • Email delivery. Many form submissions travel to the office by ordinary email. Please do not put highly sensitive details—such as health information, Social Security numbers, or full financial account numbers—in a website form. Use a phone call or another secure channel with the office when you need to share that kind of information.
  • Member accounts. If you register for a member account, we store the details you provide, such as your name, username, and email address, so you can sign in to member-only pages and resources. Member directory and household details are kept in our church membership software, Church Admin, which is provided by a third-party vendor acting as our service provider. We expect that vendor to keep member data confidential under their terms of service. Directory information is shown to other members only when someone has chosen to appear on the password-protected address list, and it is never shared outside the congregation for marketing or unrelated purposes.
  • Newsletter. If you sign up for our weekly newsletter, This Week at Emerson (homepage invitation, Ways to Connect, or Yes on the Newcomer form), we email you a link to confirm your address. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 7 days. Once you confirm, your name and email address are saved in our church records and added to our newsletter mailing list, which is sent using Mailchimp and subject to the Intuit Mailchimp Privacy Statement. Mailchimp may record whether a newsletter was opened and which links were clicked, so we can see which messages are useful. We use your email address only to send you church news and announcements. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter.

Sensitive information

Some of what we hold is sensitive in a church setting: religious affiliation or participation, pledges and giving, and children’s religious-education registration. We treat that information with extra care, limit access to people who need it for their roles, and do not use the member directory for purposes outside the congregation.

How we use your information

We use the information you share with us to respond to your questions, welcome newcomers, keep members informed, manage pledges and donations, and run the congregation’s ministries. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for ministry, legal, or security reasons, then delete or anonymize it when we can. In particular:

  • Unconfirmed newsletter sign-ups — deleted after 7 days.
  • On-site form copies in our website database — kept while useful for ministry; we aim to delete them within 24 months.
  • Office email — kept in the church’s ordinary email and records practices for as long as the conversation or ministry need continues.
  • Member accounts and Church Admin directory records — kept while you remain connected with the chapel, and for a reasonable time afterward if needed for church records, then removed or archived according to congregational practice.
  • Microsoft Forms pledge and commitment data — retained in Microsoft Forms / our office records for stewardship and membership needs; ask the office if you want a specific entry reviewed or removed.
  • Failed-login IP and username logs — kept for about 30 days for security, then discarded.

Donations

Online donations are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your full card or bank details. PayPal’s handling of your information is described in the PayPal Privacy Statement.

Cookies

Our site uses a small number of cookies that are needed for it to work, for example to keep you signed in to a member account. If you log in, WordPress sets cookies that last for two days, or two weeks if you choose “Remember Me.” Logging out removes them. We do not place advertising cookies ourselves. Embedded services such as YouTube or Maps may still set their own cookies when you use them. Details and choices are on our Cookies page.

Our homepage may show a pop-up inviting you to join our newsletter. When you close it or sign up, your browser stores a small note (in local storage, which works like a cookie) recording that choice and the date, so the pop-up stays hidden for 30 days, or permanently once you sign up. The note contains no personal information and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data, or by using the clear control on the Cookies page.

Third-party services and embedded content

Some pages include content or links from other services. Those services may collect information about you and set their own cookies when you interact with them, according to their own privacy policies:

  • Microsoft Forms — our current commitment form (see above). Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Google — YouTube sermon embeds (including privacy-enhanced embeds where we control them); the Google Maps embed on Visit Us (loaded without an API key); and any remaining Google Forms links. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Zoom — links to online Sunday services.
  • PayPal — online donations. PayPal Privacy Statement.
  • Mailchimp (Intuit) — sending the weekly newsletter after you confirm, including open and click reporting. Intuit Mailchimp Privacy Statement.
  • Church Admin — our membership and directory software, provided by a third-party vendor as a service provider.

Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. Registration for children’s religious education is handled by parents or guardians.

Keeping your information safe

Access to personal information is limited to church staff and volunteers who need it for their roles. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no website can guarantee complete security.

To protect member accounts, if a wrong password is entered several times, our website records the internet (IP) address and the username that were used, and briefly blocks further login attempts from that address. We keep that security log for about 30 days.

Your choices

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to remove you from our mailing lists. To make a request, contact the church office using the details below. We aim to respond within 60 days. We may need to verify that the request comes from you before we change or delete records.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
