Our Purpose, Mission, and Vision

Our purpose is to transform ourselves, our community, and our world.
Our mission is to provide a liberal, welcoming community that nurtures people on diverse spiritual paths through engaging worship, collaborative learning, and social action.
Part of our vision is to be a beacon of liberal religion and service that's recognized as a source for stimulating spiritual, social, and intellectual interaction.
