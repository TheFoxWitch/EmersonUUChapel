Emerson Unitarian Universalist Chapel (“Emerson Chapel,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information our website, emersonuuchapel.org, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Contact and newcomer forms. When you fill out a form on our site (for example the Newcomer Information form), we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, and your message. These submissions are sent by email to our church office. We do not keep a separate copy of every form entry in our website database unless noted below.
  • Commitment and pledge forms. Our current commitment form on the homepage is hosted by Microsoft Forms. Information you enter there is collected by Microsoft on our behalf and is subject to the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Some older pages may still link to a Google Form; those submissions are collected by Google on our behalf and are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • On-site survey and other Calculated Fields forms. Forms that open in a dialog on our site and are built with our form plugin (for example the Emerson Community Survey) send your answers by email to the church office. Those answers are not stored in Microsoft or Google Forms.
  • Member accounts. If you register for a member account, we store the details you provide, such as your name, username, and email address, so you can sign in to member-only pages and resources. Member directory and household details are kept in our church records system and are shown to other members only when someone has chosen to appear on the password-protected address list.
  • Newsletter. If you sign up for our weekly newsletter, This Week at Emerson (homepage invitation, Ways to Connect, or Yes on the Newcomer form), we email you a link to confirm your address. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 7 days. Once you confirm, your name and email address are saved in our church records and added to our newsletter mailing list, which is sent using Mailchimp and subject to the Intuit Mailchimp Privacy Statement. We use your email address only to send you church news and announcements. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter.

How we use your information

We use the information you share with us to respond to your questions, welcome newcomers, keep members informed, manage pledges and donations, and run the congregation’s ministries. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Donations

Online donations are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your full card or bank details. PayPal’s handling of your information is described in the PayPal Privacy Statement.

Cookies

Our site uses a small number of cookies that are needed for it to work, for example to keep you signed in to a member account. If you log in, WordPress sets cookies that last for two days, or two weeks if you choose “Remember Me.” Logging out removes them. We do not use advertising cookies. Details and choices are on our Cookies page.

Our homepage may show a pop-up inviting you to join our newsletter. When you close it or sign up, your browser stores a small note (in local storage, which works like a cookie) recording that choice and the date, so the pop-up stays hidden for 30 days, or permanently once you sign up. The note contains no personal information and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data, or by using the clear control on the Cookies page.

Third-party services and embedded content

Some pages include content or links from other services. Those services may collect information about you and set their own cookies when you interact with them, according to their own privacy policies:

  • Microsoft Forms — our current commitment form (see above). Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Google — YouTube sermon embeds; the Google Maps embed on Visit Us (loaded without an API key); and any remaining Google Forms links. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Zoom — links to online Sunday services.
  • PayPal — online donations. PayPal Privacy Statement.
  • Mailchimp (Intuit) — sending the weekly newsletter after you confirm. Intuit Mailchimp Privacy Statement.

Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. Registration for children’s religious education is handled by parents or guardians.

Keeping your information safe

Access to personal information is limited to church staff and volunteers who need it for their roles. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no website can guarantee complete security.

To protect member accounts, if a wrong password is entered several times, our website records the internet (IP) address and the username that were used, and briefly blocks further login attempts from that address. We keep a short log of these blocks for security purposes only.

Your choices

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to remove you from our mailing lists. To make a request, contact the church office using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
