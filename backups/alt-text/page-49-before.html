Online contributions

Click the Donate button to use PayPal for online contributions. Bank transfer (ACH) and all major credit/debit cards from Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover are accepted. Please note that PayPal charges a fee of 3% per transaction. If you would like to cover the transaction fees for Emerson Chapel, please add the fee amount to the total and select the “Contribution with fee covered” option in the “(Optional) Use this donation for” dropdown. Thank you for your contribution!
